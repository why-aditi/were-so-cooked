# Spike 1 — Vectorize on a free account

**Question (spec section 14):** Can a free account create and query a Vectorize index?

**Decides:** Vectorize vs the D1 fallback (section 12).

**Run at:** 2026-09-25T05:24:24.451Z

## Answer

**Yes.** A free account created the index, upserted 5 vectors and got 3 ranked matches back. Vectorize is usable; the D1 fallback in section 12 stays a documented contingency rather than the default. Vectors became queryable 68037 ms after upsert, so the write path is not instant — the viral pipeline must not assume read-after-write.

## What this changes

- **Storage budget:** at 1024 dimensions the free allowance of 5 million stored
  dimensions holds about 4,882 recipes.
  Section 4 assumed about 4,800.
- **Read-after-write:** 68037 ms between upsert and visibility.
  `ViralRecipesWorkflow` must treat the Vectorize upsert as fire-and-forget and keep D1 authoritative.
- **Pre-filtering:** metadata filters work, so the section 6 diet and allergen pre-filter can run inside the query.
